# KinderHub User Guide

KinderHub works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone.
You can also **install** it like an app: in Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.

## 1. Logging in

1. Open KinderHub and click **Log in**.
2. Enter your email and password, then click **Log in**.
3. The menu on the left shows only the pages for **your role**.

To log out, click **Logout** at the bottom of the menu.
Forgot your password? Use **Forgot your password?** on the login page.

**Dark mode:** click the 🌙 button at the top right. KinderHub remembers your choice.
**Live badge:** the green **● Live** badge means updates arrive automatically. If it shows **Offline**, check your internet; it reconnects by itself.

---

## 2. For parents 👨‍👩‍👧

### My children
Shows every child registered with **your email**:
* **Today**: Present / Late / Absent / Sick, with check-in and pick-up times.
* **Last 2 weeks**: one colored square per day (green = present, yellow = late, red = absent, orange = sick).
* **Daily reports**: mood 😊, meals, nap, activities and the teacher's comment.

You get a pop-up message **immediately** when your child arrives, is picked up, or gets a new report. No need to refresh.

### Announcements
News for the whole kindergarten and for your child's group. An orange number in the menu shows how many new announcements arrived.

> Don't see your child? Ask the kindergarten to check that your child is registered with the same email you use to log in.

---

## 3. For teachers 👩‍🏫

### Attendance
1. Choose the **group** and the **date** (today by default; future dates are not allowed).
2. Click **Present**, **Late**, **Absent** or **Sick** for each child. The check-in time is filled in automatically.
3. When a parent picks up a child, click **Check out**.
4. **Mark everyone not marked as present** saves time in the morning.

Parents see each change instantly.

### Daily reports
1. Choose the group and date.
2. Click **Write** next to a child (or **Edit** if the report exists).
3. Choose the mood, fill in meals, nap minutes and activities, add a comment if you like, and click **Save report**.

### Children
Search by child or parent name, filter by group, and edit a child's details with the ✏️ button.

### History & export
All attendance records, newest first. Filter by group and click **Export CSV** to download a file that opens in Excel.

### Announcements
Click **New announcement**, write a title and message, choose **Everyone** or one group, and click **Publish**. Everyone who is online gets a pop-up.

## 4. For administrators 🛡️

Administrators can do everything teachers can, plus:

| Page | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Groups** | Add, edit and delete groups. Capacity cannot be lower than the number of children; a group with children cannot be deleted. |
| **Children** | **Add child** and **delete** a child (with confirmation). A child must be 1–8 years old; a full group refuses new children. |
| **System status** | Health of the system and database, uptime, memory and server information. |

---

## 5. Messages you may see

| Message | Meaning / what to do |
|---|---|
| *The child must be at least 1 year old* | Check the date of birth. |
| *Group "…" is full* | Choose another group or increase the capacity (Admin → Groups). |
| *This group still has children* | Move the children to another group before deleting it. |
| *Check-out must be after check-in* | Correct the times. |
| *Access denied* | Your role cannot open this page. |
| *Oops, something went wrong* | Click **Try again**. If it repeats, tell the administrator. |
